PasteFix Help

Support

Answers to common purchase, permission, and clipboard questions.

Restore a purchase

Click the PasteFix menu bar icon, choose Unlock PasteFix, then Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase.

Manage or cancel the monthly subscription

Open App Store subscription settings. Apple manages charges, free trials, renewals, cancellations, and refunds.

PasteFix did not change what I copied

PasteFix skips content it considers risky to modify: sensitive-looking text such as passwords, keys, and one-time codes; content copied from excluded apps such as password managers; Universal Clipboard items; and very large clips. The menu bar shows the reason for the last skip.

Undo a change

Choose Undo Last Fix from the menu bar to restore the original clipboard contents. The restore copy is kept in memory only while PasteFix is running.

Ignore an app

Open the menu bar, choose Apps, then Ignore. PasteFix will leave clipboard content from that app untouched. You can manage the list in Settings.

Screen Recording and Accessibility permissions

These permissions are optional. Screen Recording is used only by Visual Match and Paste as Screenshot. Accessibility is used only by Paste as Screenshot, to find the selected text on screen. Grant them in System Settings, then Privacy & Security. If Paste as Screenshot cannot capture your selection, PasteFix pastes a rendered image of the copied text instead and tells you why in the menu.

Pause PasteFix

Choose Pause from the menu bar for 5 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour, or until you resume.

Need more help? Email nksnaresh@icloud.com. Include your macOS version, the app you copied from and pasted into, and a short description of what happened. Please do not send passwords or private clipboard contents.
