Privacy Policy

PasteFix processes clipboard content locally on your Mac. It does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share your data.

Data we collect

None. PasteFix has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking, and it does not send clipboard content or usage data to the developer or any third party.

Clipboard content

  • Clipboard content is read and modified locally from the macOS pasteboard.
  • PasteFix does not keep a clipboard history. A copy of the last original clipboard item is held in memory so you can undo a change; it is discarded when PasteFix quits.
  • Paste as Screenshot places captured image data on the clipboard only, so you can paste it into another app. Screenshots are not saved to disk.

Permissions

  • Screen Recording (optional) is used only for Visual Match and Paste as Screenshot, to sample the source window style or capture the selected text area. Images stay on your Mac.
  • Accessibility (optional) is used only for Paste as Screenshot, to locate the selected text on screen.
  • Launch at Login is optional and controlled from Settings.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. PasteFix receives only the entitlement status needed to unlock features and never sees your payment information.

Local diagnostics

PasteFix writes an operational log inside its sandbox container on your Mac. The log records event types, outcomes, permission state, and byte counts. It never contains copied text and is never uploaded automatically.

Preferences

Settings such as enabled rules, excluded apps, and weekly counters are stored locally using macOS user defaults.

Children

PasteFix does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
