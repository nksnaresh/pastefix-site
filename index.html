PasteFix

Clean, preserve, and format copied content before you paste it. PasteFix lives in your Mac menu bar and processes your clipboard locally.

What it does

  • Preserves rich text formatting when you paste between apps.
  • Cleans copied text: quotes, dashes, spacing, bullets, and tables.
  • Removes URL tracking parameters from copied links.
  • Converts content to Markdown, quote blocks, code blocks, and Markdown tables, and changes text case.
  • Optional Paste as Screenshot mode for when visual fidelity matters.
  • Skips sensitive-looking content and known password managers by default.
Private by design. Clipboard content is processed on your Mac and is never sent to any server.
